Coming next

What we are preparing

CITSO is in private beta. Here are the projects under way and coming next. They follow the same rules as the rest of the suite: read-only by default, nothing without your approval, and no data leaving your premises without an explicit decision.

A specialised analysis assistant

Coming next

A small artificial intelligence dedicated to Active Directory, running on your CITSO platform, with no cloud and no data sent out.

  • explain a finding in plain language, with its concrete consequences;
  • propose a fix order that takes your dependencies into account;
  • prepare summaries for management and risk committees;
  • answer questions about your directory from the latest campaign data.

It assists, it does not decide: no fix goes out without your approval, and every answer points to the evidence it relies on.

coming next

Entra ID

In progress

Extend the analysis to the cloud side of your Microsoft identity, for hybrid environments:

  • synchronisation between the directory and Entra ID, and the sync account;
  • permanent or just-in-time privileged roles;
  • applications, consents and service principals;
  • conditional access policies and strong authentication.

Read-only, with a cloud connection opened only on your decision.

in progress

AWS

Coming next

Analyse the identities and rights of your AWS accounts, and their links with the directory:

  • overly permissive IAM users, roles and policies;
  • IAM Identity Center and federation with your directory;
  • AWS Managed Microsoft AD and AD Connector;
  • paths leading from a directory identity to administrative rights in the cloud.
coming next

Google Cloud

Coming next

The same approach for Google Cloud and Cloud Identity:

  • IAM roles at organisation, folder and project level;
  • service accounts and their keys;
  • synchronisation with the directory and single sign-on;
  • escalation paths from the directory to cloud administration.
