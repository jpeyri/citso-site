Services

Two ways to work with us

In both cases, the same commitment: fix your directory with no risk to production, with a rollback for every action, and never show "compliant" on anything that was not checked. Consulting to measure and fix; the on-premises instance to hold over time.

Consulting

Know where you stand, then fix without breaking anything

Complete analysis coverage in a single campaign. CITSO brings together analysis angles usually found in separate tools:

  • directory hygiene and configuration, the ground covered by tools such as PingCastle;
  • exposure and compromise indicators, like those of Purple Knight;
  • attack paths to Tier 0, in the manner of BloodHound;
  • attack techniques mapped to the MITRE ATT&CK matrix;
  • ANSSI checkpoints and maturity levels 1 to 5.

What you receive. Nearly 400 read-only checks, the directory map, paths to Tier 0, ANSSI maturity, interactive, Word and PDF reports presented in a debrief, and a remediation plan ranked by priority.

Support. Rollout of the plan wave by wave, each fix approved with its rollback, then follow-up campaigns to confirm what is really fixed. At the end, the data is purged from the consultant's laptop and the purge is verified.

one-off or over several months

CITSO in-house

An impregnable keep, and your point of resilience if attacked

A dedicated platform, a reserved hypervisor or an appliance, runs the CITSO orchestrator, the PKI, the Red Forest and at least one replica DC per managed domain. It is invisible from production; your administrators access it only with strong authentication.

  • Point-in-time restore. Return the directory to its exact state at a given moment in the past, from the replicas and the sealed backup vault.
  • Monitoring of high-level changes. Privileged groups, delegations, GPOs linked to domain controllers, PKI, AdminSDHolder: every change is reported within seconds.
  • Dynamic invalidation. A suspicious Tier 0 change can be reverted on the spot, and the object returns to its previous state.
  • Cut-off on a suspected crisis. At the first signal, the suspect forest is isolated: links cut, sent to a black hole, forest frozen intact. The keep keeps running; release requires a signature.
  • Full restore. If production is lost, the production DCs are destroyed and the forest is rebuilt automatically from the platform, under a two-signature ceremony with CISO approval.
  • Day to day. Dashboard, maturity trend, remaining work, alerts to your SIEM.
