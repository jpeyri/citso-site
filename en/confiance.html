Our principles

Six rules

A tool that controls administrative accounts becomes a target itself. These are the rules CITSO holds itself to.

1No green without verificationA check that could not read its data is marked "not assessed", never "compliant".
2The audit writes nothingA fix is a separate operation, approved, logged, with its rollback.
3Isolate firstAt the first sign of compromise, CITSO isolates. Only a manager can lift the isolation.
4Administrators kept apartAdministrative accounts live in the Red Forest, isolated from production.
5Two signaturesRebuilding a forest or removing a controller requires two signatures and the CISO's approval.
6Everything stays with youCITSO runs with no outside connection. No directory data leaves your premises.
