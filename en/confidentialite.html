Privacy

This site sets no cookies, uses no audience measurement tool and no external font or resource, and contains no form.

If you write to us at contact@citso.eu, your message is used solely to reply to you; it is neither transferred nor sold. You may request access to your data or its deletion at any time at the same address.

GitHub's role

GitHub is used only to display this public website. It receives no data from our clients: no directory data, no audit results, no reports, no messages. CITSO, the product, runs entirely on the client's premises, offline, with no connection to GitHub or to any other online service.

Like any host, GitHub may technically log the IP addresses of visitors to this site for the security of its service, in accordance with its own policy.
