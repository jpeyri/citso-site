Secure your Active Directory.
Recover it, whatever happens.

CITSO fixes the weaknesses in your directory without breaking production. If an attack still gets through, it lets you return to a clean state or rebuild the forest. The software is French and runs entirely on your premises.

A citadel is a town's last stronghold: you fall back to it, you hold it, and from there you take back the rest. CITSO plays that role for your Active Directory.

What CITSO does

SecureCITSO audits the directory read-only and shows how an attacker could become an administrator. It then proposes fixes: each one is tested first, applied with your approval, and can be undone.
RecoverAdministrative accounts are moved to a separate forest. If the directory is compromised, CITSO isolates it, restores it to an earlier date or rebuilds the forest.
Two ways to work with us

Consulting or on premises

ConsultingA consultant audits the directory with CITSO, hands you a remediation plan and can help you carry it out.
In-houseCITSO is installed on your premises. It monitors the directory continuously and lets you recover it after an attack.
