The solution

Secure the directory, then be able to recover it

CITSO deals with Active Directory, and only with it. Most serious attacks go through the directory, and it is the directory that has to be restored afterwards.

The citadel

A separate platform,
guarding your forests.

On premises, CITSO installs a citadel on your side that monitors, protects and can rebuild everything. Your production environments trust it; the reverse does not exist.

CITSO citadel · your forests under its guardon premises

How it works

1SeeA read-only audit, aligned with ANSSI, CIS and MITRE ATT&CK. It also shows the paths an attacker would follow to become an administrator.
2RemediateEach fix is checked before it is applied, then applied with your approval. It can be undone.
3MonitorChanges to administrative accounts and groups are reported within seconds. A suspicious change can be reverted.
4RecoverIf there is an attack, the forest is isolated. It is restored to an earlier date, or rebuilt.

The Red Forest

The accounts that administer the directory are moved to a separate forest that an attacker cannot reach from production. CITSO lives there too.

Two ways to use it

With a consultant, for an audit and the fixes. Or installed on your premises on a dedicated platform, to protect the directory over time.
