The solution

From audit to crisis recovery

CITSO covers the full security of your Active Directory: understanding where it is exposed, fixing it, protecting it for the long term and, if necessary, rebuilding it.

1AuditNearly 400 checks based on the ANSSI, CIS and MITRE ATT&CK frameworks. A full campaign takes a few minutes, read-only, and every verdict comes with its evidence.
2UnderstandThe directory map, from forest down to object, coloured by severity. Attack paths to Tier 0. Maturity according to the levels published by ANSSI.
3MonitorSensitive changes to Tier 0 (privileged groups, delegations, managed service accounts, policies linked to the domain controllers) trigger an alert within seconds.
4RemediateA catalogue of remediations, each with its plan, its approval and its rollback. Outstanding work is tracked from one campaign to the next.
5ProtectThe Red Forest isolates your administrative accounts. Boundaries between zones, sealed backups and remote domain controllers complete the fortress.
6Regain controlAfter a compromise, Safe Harbour lets you isolate, restore in a separate environment and rebuild, step by step, with a guided assistant.

The architecture

CITSO and your Tier 0 accounts sit in an isolated administrative forest. Your production forests trust it through a one-way trust; the reverse does not exist.

  • Navigation from forest down to object
  • Layers: open findings, remediation progress, paths to Tier 0, unaudited zones
  • The most serious finding is two clicks away
  • A level is only validated if all its points have been verified
  • A single unverified point leaves the level “not demonstrated”
  • CITSO score out of 100 and trend from one campaign to the next

Two ways to deploy

With a consultant

  • Portable installation, one engagement per client
  • Campaign run and reports delivered on site
  • Encrypted transfer to the platform; a package that has been altered, replayed or comes from another client is rejected
  • At the end of the engagement, the data is purged and the purge is verified

In-house

  • CITSO installed in your Red Forest, connected to your forests
  • Dashboard and continuous monitoring
  • Tracking of outstanding work from one campaign to the next
  • Safe Harbour, sealed backups and remote controllers

What you need

  • A Windows workstation or server with PowerShell 5.1. For a one-off audit, it does not need to be domain-joined.
  • A standard read-only account, member of the “Event Log Readers” group for monitoring.
  • Network access to the domain controllers: LDAP (389/636), Kerberos (88), and SMB (445) to read Group Policy.
