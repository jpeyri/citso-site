A specialised analysis assistant

Coming next

A small artificial intelligence dedicated to Active Directory, running on your CITSO platform, with no cloud and no data sent out.

  • explain a finding in plain language, with its concrete consequences;
  • propose a fix order that takes your dependencies into account;
  • prepare summaries for management and risk committees;
  • answer questions about your directory from the latest campaign data.

It assists, it does not decide: no fix goes out without your approval, and every answer points to the evidence it relies on.

Microsoft Sentinel integration

Coming next

Today CITSO already sends its alerts to your SIEM over HTTPS. For Sentinel, we are preparing a native integration:

  • verdicts, Tier 0 alerts and the chained log written to a dedicated table in your Log Analytics workspace;

Domain controllers on Linux

Under study

Samba domain controllers, joined to your Active Directory forests alongside the Windows controllers. This is the "OS break" CITADELLE SOLUTIONS was aiming for back in 2016: destructive malware written for Windows, such as NotPetya, does not wipe them, and they remain available to rebuild.

  • intended use : replica DCs and recovery controllers on the CITSO platform, with no extra Windows licences;
  • functional level : Samba accepts forests at the 2016 level since version 4.19;
  • limits to respect : SYSVOL replication is not provided through DFS-R and must be handled separately; certificate services and GPO management stay on the Windows side; every version is validated in the lab before being offered.

Chosen distribution: Debian. It provides the samba-ad-dc package built with Heimdal, the Kerberos implementation with which Samba is complete as a domain controller (Red Hat distributions do not ship an AD domain controller, and the MIT Kerberos variant notably loses read-only domain controllers and PKINIT). Debian is stable, lean and community-run, with recent Samba versions in backports and a long maintenance cycle. In France, Tranquil IT also maintains Samba AD packages and reference documentation.
