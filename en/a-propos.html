About

A solution built from real crises

CITSO grew out of many engagements on compromised Active Directory environments. It was designed from the outset to run on the client's premises, without depending on any external service.

What crises have taught us

  • A directory rarely falls because of a single flaw. It falls because of permissions and delegations accumulated over the years, which nobody noticed any more.
  • During a crisis, you need to know very quickly what you can still rely on.
  • An overly reassuring dashboard costs more than an alert.
  • Without intact backups and healthy domain controllers elsewhere, you do not rebuild; you just endure.

Why everything happens on your premises

Directory data is among the most sensitive data an organisation holds. CITSO processes it in your infrastructure, with no cloud or telemetry, and its reports open offline.

How we work

Every change to CITSO is reviewed by someone who did not write it, then tested on our Active Directory lab before it reaches you.
