About

CITSO, for CITadelle SOlutions

The project was born in 2016 from work on compromised Active Directory environments.

The original idea has not changed: build a citadel around the directory, because the most serious attacks go through it. In 2016 it was an appliance that took back control of the directory and redeployed clean controllers. Today CITSO also covers auditing, monitoring and rebuilding.

The logo is a star-shaped bastioned citadel, like those built by Vauban.

What crises have taught us: a directory rarely falls because of a single flaw, but because of accumulated rights that nobody sees any more. And without intact backups and clean controllers elsewhere, you cannot rebuild.
