Getting out of a crisis: three scenarios

After a directory compromise, there are three ways out. The greater the effort, the stronger the trust you regain. The right choice depends on how far the compromise goes: we help you make it, and preparation (Safe Harbour, sealed backups, remote controllers) makes the first two scenarios realistic.
