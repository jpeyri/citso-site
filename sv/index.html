What we do

Two promises,
kept end to end.

Everything in CITSO serves two goals: make the directory hard to take, and be able to recover it intact if someone manages to anyway.

1 · Secure

A hardened directory, without breaking production

  • Risk analysis before any fix : CITSO finds what still depends on weak settings, then proposes an action plan in the right order.
  • Fixes with rollback, applied in waves, only with your approval, and verified by checks before and after.
  • Tier 0 kept safe in the Red Forest, an isolated administration forest; your administrators access it with strong authentication.
  • Monitoring of sensitive changes : privileged groups, delegations, GPOs, PKI. A suspicious change can be reverted immediately.
2 · Recover

Roll back, or rebuild everything

  • Immediate cut-off at the first sign of compromise: the suspect forest is isolated and frozen intact; nothing is erased.
  • Point-in-time restore from the replica DCs (at least one per domain) and the sealed backup vault.
  • Automated rebuild of the forest from the citadel if production is lost, with two signatures and CISO approval.
  • Safe Harbour : a shelter prepared in advance, with isolation plans rehearsed regularly.

Problemet

Active Directory is the key to everything,
and ransomware's first target.

Whoever takes it takes everything. CISOs know their directory is fragile, yet it rarely gets fixed. For three reasons:

1Years of accumulated settingsForgotten rights, inherited delegations, over-powerful service accounts: the debt has built up, and no one has the full picture any more.
2Fear of breaking productionHardening the directory can break an application, a service, an authentication flow. Without visibility on the impact, people prefer not to touch it.
3Tools that list without fixingAudits produce lists of weaknesses. They don't say how to fix them safely, or how to roll back if something breaks.
What CITSO does

How CITSO fixes,
in four steps.

1Audit and monitor, read-onlyChecks are based on the ANSSI, CIS and MITRE ATT&CK frameworks. Nothing is written to the directory during the audit or during monitoring.
2Show the attack pathsCITSO maps the paths that lead to administrator accounts and gives a fix order: what to cut first.
3Fix with your approvalFixes run under lock, only when you approve them, and each one has its rollback ready. Actions that cannot be undone are blocked by default.
4Prove every verdictEvery finding comes with its evidence. Anything that could not be checked stays "not assessed": CITSO never shows "compliant" by default.

Det vi bygger upp

CITSO:s resurser, samlade på en gemensam grund

The orchestrator, the PKI, the replica DCs (at least one per managed domain), the backups, forensic analysis and alerts are brought together in the administration forest. Your forests connect to it through a one-way trust or a VPN, depending on your architecture.

On premises: the fortress

Sex försvarslinjer för att stå emot en attack och resa sig efter en kris

Installed on your premises, CITSO stops an attacker from reaching your most powerful accounts and, if they get there, keeps everything needed to rebuild.

Red ForestThe administration forest hosts CITSO and your Tier 0 accounts. It is not joined to any production forest. Your forests trust it; the reverse does not exist.
GränserSeparata administrationszoner, begränsade grupper, förtroenden med selektiv autentisering och SID-filtrering. Ett komprometterat konto i produktionsmiljön når inte upp till administrationen.
Övervakning av Tier 0Ändringar som berör Tier 0 rapporteras inom några sekunder. Om en signal om kompromettering dyker upp isolerar CITSO omedelbart. Isoleringen hävs först när en ansvarig har signerat.
Safe HarbourA shelter prepared in advance: isolation plans tested regularly, replicas kept apart, guided rebuild. During the crisis, nothing is erased.
Förseglade säkerhetskopiorSäkerhetskopiorna av administrationsskogen och de skyddade skogarna är krypterade, och deras integritet kontrolleras med hashvärden. Den som skulle kontrollera produktionsmiljön kan inte nå dem.
Replica DCsAt least one replica domain controller per managed domain, safe on the platform and checked before it becomes too old to use. Enough to restore to a point in time or rebuild everything.

How attacks get through

Rarely through a spectacular flaw

Oftast är det en kedja av detaljer: en bortglömd behörighet att återställa lösenord, ett tjänstkonto med för långtgående delegering, en administratörssession som lämnats öppen på en server. Var för sig inget alarmerande. Tillsammans leder de till domänadministratörerna.

No green without proof

Det som inte kunde verifieras redovisas som sådant

A check that could not read the data it needs does not appear as compliant. The example opposite comes from a campaign in our lab, run with a standard read account.

Kontot hade inte åtkomst till den delade mappen för grupprinciper. De berörda kontrollerna förblev ”ej utvärderade”, med angiven orsak.
