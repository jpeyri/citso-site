Pilot programme

Shall we look at your directory together?

CITSO is in private beta. We are opening a pilot programme to a few organisations: CISOs and Active Directory owners at mid-sized companies, regulated operators and the public sector, consultancies and IT service firms.

contact@citso.eu

How the pilot works

  1. A demo on a test environment.
  2. A first read-only campaign on your directory: nothing is written to it.
  3. The debrief: attack paths, evidence-backed verdicts, fix order.
  4. If you wish, supported fixes, each with its rollback.

A few lines are enough to start: the size of your directory (number of forests, domains, accounts), what worries you, and whether you are thinking of a one-off audit or a lasting installation.
